# Curated Scientific Datasets for Labeling & Accuracy Benchmarks

| Dataset Name | Source / Organization | Application in Accuracy Benchmarking | Access Link |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **SciERC** | Allen Institute for AI / University of Washington | Benchmark for testing named entity recognition, relation extraction, and coreference resolution accuracy between AI models and manual annotators. | [SciERC Dataset](http://nlp.cs.washington.edu/sciIE/) |
| **NCBI Disease Corpus** | National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI) | Gold-standard corpus of 793 PubMed abstracts double-annotated by biomedical experts; used to evaluate token-level span precision and boundary errors in automated annotation. | [NCBI Disease Portal](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/CBBresearch/Dogan/DISEASE/) |
| **MIMIC-IV-Note** | PhysioNet / MIT-LCP | Over 300,000 de-identified clinical notes used to evaluate active-learning efficiency and measure reduction in clinician manual labeling workloads. | [PhysioNet MIMIC-IV](https://physionet.org/content/mimic-iv-note/) |
